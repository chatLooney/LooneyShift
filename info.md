# LooneyShift — Privacy and support

LooneyShift's photo-cleaning and IPA-inspection tools perform processing locally on the device. Imported files remain under the user's control and are not uploaded by these tools. Location simulation uses the device connection and pairing setup; users should not share pairing records or signing credentials.

LooneyShift is provided as-is for compatible iPhones. Device behaviour and compatibility vary; consult [VALIDATION.md](VALIDATION.md) for limitations.
